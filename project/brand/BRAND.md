# Pickle Castle Game Studio — brand reference

**Studio:** Pickle Castle Game Studio
**Mascot:** King Pickles — a crowned pug atop a pickle-green castle, red royal cape, pickle scepter. (The castle = Castle; the pug king = Pickles. The pug is also Zone 1's boss in THE SHATTERED ARCHIVE.)
**Tagline:** Games that inspire adventure, curiosity & joy.

## Color palette
| Role | Hex |

| Cream | `#FFE6B3` |

## Logo assets (extracted from the brand sheet)
- **Hero** — full castle + King Pickles + PICKLE CASTLE / GAME STUDIO wordmark, on a dark-green vignette (corner ≈ `#060c0e`). Used as the in-game boot splash (studio ident).
- **Primary** — compact stacked crowned-pug + wordmark.
- **Circle icon** — mascot in a green ring (good for favicons / small marks).
- Alternate one-color, black & white, wordmark-only, and shield emblem variants also exist on the sheet.

## Usage rules (locked with creator)
- In THE SHATTERED ARCHIVE, studio brand colors are **quarantined** to the boot splash (and credits if a studio line is added). The game world keeps its own locked purple/dark-fantasy palette — never bleed studio green/orange into gameplay or menus.
- The splash uses the real painterly logo (raster), deliberately higher-fidelity than the game's pixel art — standard for a studio ident. It's compressed (WebP ~93KB) and shown once per cold boot, skippable.
- For future Pickle Castle games, this palette + mascot + tagline are the studio's consistent identity; each game keeps its own art direction for its world.
