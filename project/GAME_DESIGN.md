# THE SHATTERED ARCHIVE (fka Claude Quest) — Design Doc v1.0 (2026-07-16)

**Concept.** A SNES-era top-down RPG that turns you into an expert on the entire Claude ecosystem. The great Archive of Capability has fragmented into 8 corrupted zones; each zone teaches one discipline and is guarded by a **Misconception** — a boss embodying the most common wrong belief about that discipline. Defeat all 8, restore the Archive, earn the title **Archivist**.

**Pillars** (every decision must serve these):
1. **Learning IS the power system.** All player power (damage, HP, gear, specials) comes from demonstrated knowledge — never grinding.
2. **Respect the player's mind.** Evidence-based learning: retrieval practice, spaced review, immediate feedback, reteach-on-miss. Failure teaches; it never humiliates or hard-blocks.
3. **Authentic 16-bit charm.** SNES-fidelity pixel art, juicy feedback, a world worth restoring.

**Core verb (the toy — FUN GATE PASSED v4, confirmed via real iPhone playtest 2026-07-16):** *Answer under pressure, spend Insight wisely.* Turn-based knowledge combat: every correct answer banks +1 Insight ◆ and opens the technique menu — Tome Strike (1◆), Insight Lance (3◆ beam), or Focus (bank it, but your guard drops: boss grazes ~6 HP). Streak ≥3 arms Insight Surge (doubles next damaging technique, only discharged by damage — so banking preserves it). Misses cost 15 HP, reset streak, trigger a click-to-dismiss reteach, and requeue the question; answering it right later grants a +10 **redemption heal**. Mega Man progression: each defeated Misconception's technique is purified into the player's kit. **TOTAL RECALL finisher:** misses requeue, so emptying the whole question pool = every question answered correctly at least once; if the boss still stands, the apprentice unleashes a cinematic triple-beam finisher — total mastery is the game's most spectacular moment, tracked in save stats. Sim-verified mathematically unreachable in the base game (boss dies first; pacifist play dies to grazes) — by design. **Full Guard (Echo Cycles only, locked 2026-07-16):** at 5◆ Insight, Focus provokes no graze, opening a deliberate high-skill finisher line (sim: ~93-99% success at 95% accuracy, but an 80%-accuracy attempt dies more often than not). Never incentivizes wrong answers. Mentor lore should foreshadow it in the base game. **Doubt Coils (Echo only, re-tuned to 28%/question after creator saw too few; Full Guard blunts the pierce to HALF graze):** the boss loudly 'coils with Doubt' before a question (red flash + aura — unmissable). Focusing into it: unguarded = ×2 crit graze; **even Full Guard is pierced** (blunted to a normal graze). Attacking disperses it but spends finisher progress. Crits never apply to miss-punishment. Sim: masters land the finisher ~28-31% — genuinely legendary. **Clarity Mend:** 5◆ heal 8 (always < redemption), disabled while coiled. **Echo Rites:** an interstitial before each cycle teaches coils, Full Guard, and the TOTAL RECALL legend so the finisher is never undiscoverable.

**World structure (BUILT v0.7.0 — all 8 zones walkable):** 8 themed halls (bookshelf Vestibule, orb-lit Hall of Memory, stalactite Caverns, sky-windowed Bridge Wing, bannered Keep, furnace-glow Codeforge, tool-pegboard Atelier, gear-walled Spire), each with its mentor NPC sprite, lore chest foreshadowing the zone's misconception, themed props, and the pet-boss seal in the zone's color. Data-driven THEMES config; spawn safe-zone enforced. Per zone: NPC mentors (micro-lessons, one concept each), practice duels (low-stakes retrieval), lore chests, an optional **Field Quest** (real-world hybrid practice: "actually do X in Claude, report back" → bonus gear), then the zone boss (mastery check, ~80% bar + a multi-step scenario reconstruction phase). Wandering **Echoes** in later zones re-test earlier material = spaced repetition.

**Audience & scope:** Creator + shareable; general ecosystem curriculum; 5–8h full playthrough; desktop + mobile web.

**Art direction:** 16-bit SNES era via sprite-artist. Fixed internal 480×270, integer scaling, nearest-neighbor. Palette locked in SPRITE_STYLE_GUIDE.json.

**Tech:** Vite + TypeScript + Phaser 3.90 (pinned). Pure game/curriculum logic separated from render. Ships as a single self-contained HTML file.

**Echo difficulty (locked):** every question carries 3 authored near-miss `hardChoices` that replace the base distractors in Echo Cycles — first playthrough stays approachable, every Echo demands sharper discrimination. **Data & freshness:** All lessons/questions live in versioned JSON content files (`content/zoneN.json`, `contentVersion` field), fact-checked against official docs at authoring time — game code never hardcodes facts, so Claude can refresh content without touching the engine.

**Pet companions (v0.12.0, locked):** every freed pet becomes a selectable companion (♥ Companions on the Archive Map) — it trots beside you through the halls and answers for you when you DELEGATE in battle. Default companion remains the other apprentice. The pug's name is Pickles (credits/UI), a family matching set with the apprentice.

**Echo Wanderers (v0.12.0, locked):** once any zone is restored, a lavender wisp drifts through every hall — communing with it starts a quick 3-question practice duel drawn from *cleared* zones, preferring the journal's still-open misconceptions (spaced repetition aimed at your actual weaknesses). Pure practice by creator choice: no combat reward — duels mend the journal and sharpen recall, nothing else. One duel per wisp; another drifts in on your next visit.

**Journal of Misconceptions (v0.11.0, locked):** every battle miss is logged persistently (keyed to the question's home zone + concept); any later correct answer — in battle or via the journal's stakes-free re-practice — mends the entry. Mended pages stay, marked gold: the journal becomes a record of every doubt conquered. Accessible from the Archive Map with an open-entry count badge. Pure pillar 2: retrieval practice with visible progress, failure as curriculum.

**Persistence:** localStorage autosave (schema-versioned, migration-safe: new fields always defaulted in loadState) + export/import save code for portability.

**Error log:** global error/rejection capture + state-anomaly checks → ring buffer in save data → one-click export of structured JSON (log + save snapshot + versions) designed for Claude ingestion/diagnosis.

**Prestige — Echo Cycles (locked 2026-07-16, scaling Monte-Carlo-tuned same day):** After restoring the Archive, it *resounds* — the whole world replays as an Echo Cycle, each cycle harder. Per cycle: boss HP +10% (capped at cycle 5, max ×1.61), boss hit +1 (cap +5), graze +1 (cap +2), redemption heal +2/cycle. Verified playable at every cycle across 70-95% accuracy players (worst case ~49%, learning players 93%+). Boss question pools become **interleaved** (~30% drawn from OTHER zones — cross-discipline retrieval, the strongest retention tool we have). Mentor lessons become optional (skippable, already learned). Reteach-on-miss NEVER goes away (pillar 2). Cycle count shown as a title suffix (Archivist II, III…); save data tracks highest cycle. Soft cap: scaling continues but cosmetic prestige rewards (palette variants, title glyphs) top out around cycle 5 so it never becomes a grind treadmill.

**Boss techniques (v0.10.0, pair-designed & locked):** Mega Man progression realized — defeating each Misconception purifies its power into a once-per-fight technique that is the misconception *inverted*: z1 Clear the Fog (2◆ burn a wrong choice), z2 Choji's Beckon (2◆ recall the mentor's lesson), z3 Delegate (3◆ companion answers — no Insight gained; delegation completes work, not learning), z4 Scrapbook (1◆ swap the question), z5 Conduit (free: 3 answers at +2◆/−1◆), z6 Pack Tactics (3◆, damage per distinct technique used), z7 Golden Thread (free: repeat last attack), z8 Scheduled Task (3◆, detonates unattended after 3 questions). Question-phase kit renders as a strip above the panel; menu techniques form column 2 of the technique menu. Unlocks persist across Echo Cycles.

**Doubt Shields (v0.15.0, built & sim-tuned):** wounded Misconceptions harden — at ⅔ and ⅓ HP (base zones 6–8, every Echo fight) a violet shield rises; small blows glance off for 1, while a heavy hit (≥26: the Lance, a streak-4 strike, anything surged) shatters it and lands in full. Monte-Carlo verified: shield-aware play is strictly faster at every accuracy level — the charged-Lance line is finally correct, closing the Strike-spam optimality gap. The TOTAL RECALL finisher ignores shields: total mastery answers to nothing.

**Pet bosses (locked 2026-07-16 — ALL EIGHT, photo-referenced from the creators' real pets):** the pug (unnamed, creator's choice) as The Vague One, Choji as The Amnesiac (maneki-neko pose — welcomes everyone as a stranger), Chewie the Pekingese as The Micromanager (crown + checklist), Scraps as The Copy-Paster (green shirt + eternal blep; shuffles 'scraps' of text), Struggles as The Silo Warden (curled in a silo-bowl), Troubles the black German Shepherd as The Lone Coder (one ear flopped), Kitherine as The Reinventor (unraveling the Atelier's gold thread), and Juno the Pomeranian as Entropy Prime (static floof corona). Victory lines free each named pet from its Misconception ('Beneath the mask, X shakes free and grins').

**Player characters (locked):** **Castle** (creator's likeness: dark beard, glasses, swept hair, navy dotted shirt) and **Pickles** (wife's likeness: copper spiral curls, translucent cat-eye glasses, wine-red velvet) — both playable, chosen at new game; the other becomes a story companion NPC. **Audio (v0.9.0, locked):** 100% runtime-synthesized Web Audio — zero audio files, zero licensing exposure (commercial requirement). Grammar: good outcomes = sine/square ascending, danger = sawtooth descending + noise (SOUND_STYLE_GUIDE.json). Ambient 2-voice chiptune loops (overworld transposes per zone / battle / celebration), persistent per-scene mute toggle. **Onboarding (v0.9.0):** teach-through-play one-time pop-ins — movement on first hall visit; first question, tech menu, surge, miss, redemption, and mend in the first fight — never a text-wall; Echo rules stay in the Echo Rites interstitial.

**Accessibility (v0.14.0, locked):** WCAG-AA contrast audited across the palette; no information carried by color alone (glyphs everywhere); no time pressure by design; text auto-fit plus an optional LARGE text mode; reduced-motion mode (flashes/shakes/strobes cut, every cue retained via aura/text/sound); full keyboard play including all menu scenes (visible focus ring); padded touch targets; persistent per-save options (⚙ from title and map). Formal HEP/PLAY rubric grade at 1.0-candidate: Story 4 · Play 4 · Mechanics 3 (accepted, mitigation designed) · Usability 4.
